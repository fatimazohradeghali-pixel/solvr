FAQ
Questions answered.
Is it safe to follow Solvr's advice? ↓

Yes. Solvr is designed with safety first. For every problem it assesses the risk. Gas leaks, major electrical faults, structural problems — it tells you immediately to call a professional. It never gives advice that could put you at risk.

What languages does it support? ↓

24+ global languages including Turkish, French, Arabic, English, Spanish, German, Russian, Chinese, Hindi, Portuguese and more. Pro and Family plans also include regional dialects — Darija (Moroccan Arabic), Gulf Arabic, Tunisian Arabic, and others.

What kinds of problems can it fix? ↓

Electricity (tripped breakers, dead outlets), plumbing (blockages, leaks, low pressure), AC and heating, WiFi problems, home appliances, doors and locks, walls and floors, gas and water heaters. More categories added regularly.

What if it can't fix my problem? ↓

Solvr tells you honestly when a problem needs a professional — and helps you understand what type of specialist you need and what to tell them, so you don't get overcharged.

Do I need technical knowledge? ↓

Zero. Solvr explains everything in plain language like a knowledgeable friend. No jargon, no complicated terms. Just clear steps anyone can follow.

Can I cancel anytime? ↓

Yes, anytime. No contracts, no lock-in. Cancel from your account in one click. You keep access until end of billing period.
